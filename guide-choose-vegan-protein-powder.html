How to choose a vegan protein powder

Vegan protein powders come in several types. This checklist helps you compare them.

Step 1: Pick the protein source

  • Soy: a complete protein on its own.
  • Pea: high in protein; usually combined with another source.
  • Rice: often blended with pea to round out the amino acids.
  • Blends: commonly pea plus rice, sometimes with seeds such as pumpkin or sunflower.

Step 2: Check protein per serving

Look for the grams of protein in one scoop and the scoop weight. A higher number is not always better value, so move on to step 4.

Step 3: Read the ingredients

A short list is easier to judge. Look at sweeteners, flavourings, gums and any added extras you did not ask for. Check for allergens such as soy.

Step 4: Work out the price per gram of protein

Divide the tub price by the total grams of protein in the tub.

Step 5: Look for independent testing

Some brands publish lab reports. It is a good sign when a company shares them.

Taste tip

Plant powders can taste earthy. Mixing with milk alternatives, banana or cocoa often helps. Try a small pack before buying a large tub.
