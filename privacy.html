Privacy Policy

This page explains what information is collected when you use Pick My Protein and how it's used.

No accounts required

You don't need an account to use this site. The protein calculator runs in your browser, and the answers you enter aren't sent to or stored on a server.

Hosting and technical data

The site's hosting provider automatically logs technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request. This is used to keep the site running securely and reliably.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use cookies and may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use it, such as which pages are popular. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Affiliate links

The site contains affiliate links to online retailers. When you click one, that retailer may set its own cookies and collect information under its own privacy policy.

Your information

We do not sell your personal information.

If you email us, your email and message are kept only as long as needed to respond and deal with your request.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy
