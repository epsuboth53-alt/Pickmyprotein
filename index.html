How your target is worked out

Your goal sets a starting point between about 1.0 and 1.8 grams of protein per kg of body weight. Your activity level then nudges that number up or down, and the result is kept between 0.8 and 2.2 g per kg. If you are 65 or older, the target is at least 1.2 g per kg.

The powder match compares your diet and your top priority (value, taste, clean label or fast recovery) against six types: whey concentrate, whey isolate, micellar casein, egg white, pea and rice blend, and soy isolate. The calorie figure is a rough estimate from your weight, activity level and goal, so treat it as a starting point. Your flavour preference (unflavoured or flavoured) decides which products are shown first.
